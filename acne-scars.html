TYPES OF CONCERNS

Types of Acne Scars

Ice Pick Scars

Deep, narrow scars that create small, pointed openings in the skin. These scars extend into deeper layers and can make the skin appear pitted.

Boxcar Scars

Broad, depressed scars with well-defined edges that create a crater-like appearance. They commonly appear on the cheeks and temples.

Rolling Scars

Scars with sloping edges that create an uneven, wave-like appearance across the skin. They are often associated with changes in the deeper skin tissue.

Hypertrophic & Keloid Scars

Raised and lumpy scars that develop when the skin produces excess collagen during healing. They appear elevated above the surrounding skin.

Post-Acne Marks

Flat brown, red, or dark marks that remain after acne heals. These are changes in skin colour rather than structural scars.

Uneven Skin Texture

A combination of shallow scars, visible pores, roughness, and surface irregularities that can make the skin appear less smooth.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about acne scars and acne scar treatments.

What are acne scars?
+
Acne scars are changes in the skin's texture or structure that develop after acne-related inflammation and healing. They may appear as pits, depressions, raised areas, or uneven skin texture.
What causes acne scars?
+
Acne scars develop when inflammation damages the skin during the healing process. Changes in collagen production may lead to depressed scars or raised scars.
What are the different types of acne scars?
+
Common types include ice pick scars, boxcar scars, rolling scars, and hypertrophic scars. Some people also experience post-acne marks and uneven skin texture.
Are acne marks and acne scars the same?
+
No. Acne marks are generally flat colour changes, such as red or brown spots. Acne scars involve structural changes in the skin, such as depressions or raised areas.
Can acne scars be improved?
+
The appearance of many acne scars can be improved with suitable procedures. The extent of improvement depends on scar type, depth, skin condition, and individual response.
Can acne scars occur even after acne has healed?
+
Yes. Scars may remain after active acne has settled because the skin's healing process can leave changes in collagen and tissue structure.
Can I have different types of acne scars at the same time?
+
Yes. Many people have a combination of ice pick, boxcar, rolling scars, post-acne marks, and uneven skin texture. Each type may require a different approach.
How many sessions are required to improve acne scars?
+
The number of sessions depends on the type and depth of scars, selected procedure, skin condition, and individual response. A personalised plan is recommended after assessment.
Do acne scar procedures have downtime?
+
Downtime varies depending on the procedure. Some options may cause mild redness, while laser resurfacing may involve greater sensitivity, peeling, redness, and recovery time.
Can new acne cause more scars?
+
Yes. New or recurring acne can lead to additional scarring, particularly when breakouts are inflamed or picked at. Managing active acne is important to help prevent further scar formation.
