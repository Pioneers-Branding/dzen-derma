THE TREATMENT

Understanding Botox

Botox is a brand of botulinum toxin type A, a neuromodulator that temporarily reduces the activity of targeted muscles by interrupting the nerve signals responsible for muscle contraction.

This helps soften dynamic wrinkles, refine selected facial features and, through specialised techniques, address certain skin-quality and excessive sweating concerns. It is not a filler and does not add volume to the face. Instead, the solution reduces the appearance of wrinkles by relaxing the movement beneath the skin.

Its effects are temporary, with results and duration varying according to the treatment area and individual response.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Botox take to show results?
+
Visible results usually start from around day 3, with the full effect generally seen by day 14. Timing varies by treatment area and individual response.
How long do Botox results last?
+
Results typically last around 6 months, though this varies by individual, treatment area and muscle activity.
Will Botox make my face look frozen or expressionless?
+
Our objective is not to eliminate every line or alter natural expression. Dosage and injection points are planned around your anatomy and muscle activity to achieve subtle, balanced results that preserve individuality.
Is Botox painful?
+
Injections are administered using a fine needle, and most people feel only brief, mild discomfort. The procedure usually takes around 15–30 minutes, depending on the areas treated.
What is the difference between Botox and fillers?
+
Botox relaxes the movement of targeted muscles to soften dynamic wrinkles. It is not a filler and does not add volume to the face. Fillers restore volume and contour, and the two are commonly used together for a comprehensive approach to facial rejuvenation.
Can Botox be used for jawline slimming?
+
Yes. Masseter reduction may reduce prominent masseter muscle bulk for a slimmer-looking jawline. Suitability is assessed during your consultation.
Can Botox help with excessive underarm and palm sweating?
+
Yes. Botox can be used for underarm hyperhidrosis and palmar (palm) hyperhidrosis, helping manage excessive sweating in the treated areas.
Is there any downtime after Botox?
+
There is no downtime in most cases. Some people may notice temporary redness, swelling or mild tenderness at the injection sites.
Is Botox safe?
+
At D’Zen Derma, Botulinum Toxin A is administered exclusively by qualified medical specialists using established, authentic brands. Certain medical conditions, medications, pregnancy or breastfeeding may affect eligibility, so your suitability is assessed at consultation.
Can I apply makeup or go to the gym right after getting Botox treatment?
+
You are usually advised to avoid applying makeup over the injection sites and to avoid strenuous exercise for the rest of the day. Your doctor will give you specific aftercare guidance after treatment.
Is Botox suitable for men?
+
Yes. Botox is suitable for both men and women, and treatment is planned around individual anatomy and goals.
How long does a Botox appointment take?
+
The procedure usually takes around 15–30 minutes, depending on the areas being treated.
Will I need a follow-up after Botox?
+
Your doctor may recommend a review after approximately two weeks to assess your response.
