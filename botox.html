THE TREATMENT

Understanding Botox

Botox is a brand of botulinum toxin type A, a neuromodulator that temporarily reduces targeted muscle activity by interrupting the nerve signals that make them contract.

Lines that appear when you frown, squint or raise your eyebrows are called dynamic wrinkles. Relaxing the muscle underneath softens them. Lines that stay visible at rest may improve only partly and sometimes need other treatments alongside.

Botox is not a filler and adds no volume to the face. Fillers restore lost volume, while Botox reduces movement, so many people plan the two together.

Anti-wrinkle injections wear off gradually as nerve signals return, so the effect is temporary and treatment can be repeated if you want to maintain it. Results typically last around 6 months, although this varies by individual, treatment area, dose, and response.

For the jawline, Botox is placed in the masseter, the chewing muscle at the corner of the jaw. As the muscle relaxes and reduces in bulk over several weeks, the lower face can look slimmer. For sweating, small amounts are placed in the skin of the underarms or palms to reduce the signals that activate sweat glands.

SAFETY & RECOVERY

Risks, Side Effects & Recovery

Side effects are usually mild and temporary:

Redness, swelling or tenderness at injection sites
Localised muscle weakness or heaviness
Headache, in rare cases

Certain medical conditions and medicines, as well as pregnancy and breastfeeding, may affect whether Botox is suitable for you. Tell your doctor about these at your consultation.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Botox and your treatment.

How long does Botox take to show results?
+
You may notice a change from around day 3. The full effect usually shows by day 14.
How long do Botox results last?
+
Botox results typically last around 6 months, although duration varies depending on the treated area and individual response.
Will Botox make my face look frozen?
+
That can happen with excessive dosing or poor placement. We aim to preserve natural movement and expression while softening targeted lines.
Is Botox painful?
+
It is a quick treatment using a very fine needle, and most people experience only brief pricks.
What is the difference between Botox and fillers?
+
Botox relaxes muscles to soften lines caused by movement. Fillers add volume where the face has lost it.
Can Botox be used for jawline slimming?
+
Yes. It relaxes the masseter muscle, and the lower face may look slimmer over the following weeks.
Can Botox help with excessive underarm and palm sweating?
+
Yes. Botox can reduce sweating in treated areas for several months, and treatment can be repeated as needed.
Is there any downtime after Botox?
+
Most people return to normal activities the same day. Small marks or mild swelling at injection sites usually settle quickly.
Is Botox safe?
+
When administered by a qualified doctor at an appropriate dose, Botox is widely used. Like any medical procedure, it carries risks, and your doctor will assess whether it is suitable for you.
Can I apply makeup or go to the gym right after?
+
Follow your doctor’s advice. Avoid rubbing the treated areas and strenuous exercise for the rest of the day, as advised.
At what age can I start Botox?
+
There is no single ideal age. Suitability depends on facial muscle movement, visible lines and individual needs. Your doctor will advise if treatment is unnecessary.
Do I need to keep having it forever?
+
No. If you stop, muscle movement gradually returns and your lines return to their natural appearance over time.
How much does Botox cost?
+
Cost depends on the areas treated and the units required. Your doctor can provide an estimate following your assessment.
Is Botox cheaper than fillers?
+
Costs vary depending on the areas and amount used. Botox and fillers address different concerns, so the recommendation depends on your facial needs.
Is 40 too late to start Botox?
+
No. Botox can soften expression lines at 40 and beyond, although deeper lines at rest may require additional treatments.
What are the side effects of Botox?
+
Redness, swelling or tenderness at injection sites are common. Temporary headache or muscle heaviness may also occur.
Can men have Botox?
+
Yes. Men can have Botox, and treatment may require different dosing and placement due to differences in facial muscle strength.
