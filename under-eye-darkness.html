CLINICAL OVERVIEW

Under-Eye Darkness

Rested-looking eyes. Softer contours. Renewed confidence.

Under eye darkness is one of the most frequently misdiagnosed. Most people have already tried concealer, cold compresses, and every eye cream promising a fix, only to find the shadow returns by evening — because under eye dark circles treatment aimed at the wrong cause was never going to hold. The skin here is the thinnest on the face, which is exactly why it shows structural change, fluid retention, and years of poor sleep faster than anywhere else.

It can make the eyes look tired, shadowed, or less refreshed, even when you feel well-rested.

Under-eye hollowness may be influenced by genetics, facial anatomy, age-related volume loss, changes in fat distribution, and skin thinning.

As a part of our commitment at D’Zen Derma, we assess the under-eye area, skin quality, facial structure (mid face), and individual concerns to recommend tailored treatment plan that helps soften hollow contours and create a more refreshed appearance.

TYPES OF CONCERNS

Types of Under-Eye Darkness

Tear Trough Hollowness

A visible groove or depression extending from the inner corner of the eye along the lower eyelid, creating a hollow or shadowed appearance.

Volume-Related Hollowness

Loss or reduced distribution of volume beneath the eyes that can make the area appear sunken and less supported.

Age-Related Under-Eye Hollowness

Hollowness that develops gradually with ageing due to changes in facial fat, skin elasticity, and underlying support structures.

Hollow Eyes with Dark Circles

A combination of under-eye indentation and pigmentation or shadows that can make the area appear darker, tired, or less even.

Wrinkles and Crows Feet

Fan-shaped lines radiating from the outer corners of the eyes, caused by repeated contraction of the muscle around the eye during smiling and squinting. They tend to appear earlier in those with thinner skin or significant sun exposure.

CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about under-eye darkness, hollowness and treatment.

What causes under-eye hollowness?
+
Under-eye hollowness can be caused by genetics, facial anatomy, age-related volume loss, changes in fat distribution, and thinning of the skin beneath the eyes.
Why do my eyes look sunken or tired even when I sleep well?
+
The appearance of tired eyes may be related to natural under-eye anatomy, volume distribution, shadows, pigmentation, or skin thinning rather than lack of sleep.
Is under-eye hollowness the same as dark circles?
+
No. Hollowness refers to a depression or loss of volume beneath the eyes, while dark circles refer to darker colouring in the under-eye area. However, hollowness can create shadows that make dark circles appear more prominent.
Can under-eye hollowness be improved?
+
Depending on its cause, the appearance of under-eye hollowness may be improved with selected procedures that address volume, skin quality, or associated concerns. Suitability depends on individual anatomy and assessment.
Is under-eye hollowness caused by ageing?
+
Ageing can contribute to under-eye hollowness through changes in facial fat, skin elasticity, and underlying support. However, it can also be present in younger individuals due to genetics and natural facial structure.
Can under-eye hollowness occur at a young age?
+
Yes. Some people naturally have deeper tear troughs or less volume beneath the eyes due to their facial anatomy and genetics.
How many sessions are required to improve under-eye hollowness?
+
The number of sessions depends on the selected procedure, degree of hollowness, skin quality, and individual goals. Some options may involve one session, while others may require multiple sessions.
Are procedures for under-eye hollowness safe?
+
The under-eye area is delicate and contains important blood vessels and structures. All procedures require careful assessment and an experienced medical professional. Each procedure has its own risks, and suitability should be determined during consultation.
Does under-eye hollowness cause dark circles?
+
Hollowness can create shadows beneath the eyes, making the area appear darker. Pigmentation, visible blood vessels, and skin thinning can also contribute to dark circles.
Can under-eye hollowness return after improvement?
+
Natural ageing and changes in facial volume continue over time. The longevity of improvement depends on the procedure, individual anatomy, and natural ageing process.
